McCrack-COS — a free desktop operating system in your browser

McCrack-COS is a browser-based desktop OS. Open it in any modern browser and you get a full desktop: a taskbar, windows you can drag and resize, a lock screen, and an app store called McCrack Market with apps, games and websites.

What you can do in McCrack-COS

  • Play unblocked games in the browser, including Eaglercraft Minecraft (1.5, 1.8.8, 1.12 and modpacks).
  • Browse the web with a built-in browser and proxy.
  • Chat with an AI assistant (McCrack AI and Jarvis).
  • Store files in McCrack Cloud cloud storage.
  • Listen to music with Spicetify and watch movies in CineOS.
  • Use a Terminal, calculator, Discord, Messages and McCrack Studio.
  • Customise everything: live video wallpapers, themes, fonts and a macOS-style desktop.

McCrack-COS is free, needs no installation, and runs on Windows, macOS, Linux, Chromebook and mobile. The official site is https://xkznn.github.io/McCrack-COS/.
